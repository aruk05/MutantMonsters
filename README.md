# MutantMonsters - 7 Days to Die Mod

Adds new mutant monsters to the game. Server-side only - Players do not need to download! 

They currently show up rarely at night and start to appear in screamer and bloodmoon hordes after gamestage 100 (increasingly so as your gamestage increases). You may want to change how hard they hit, how much life they have, and what their rewards are in order to fit your specific server, but they have some devastating abilities so make sure you prepare for how to best handle the encounter and live to tell the tale!

We have a ton of other unique monsters on our server and we may release more for the community in the coming months! Come see what we have if you don't want to wait.

## Features
* **Server-Side Only:** No client downloads required. Players can join your server and see the monsters immediately.
* **Dynamic Spawns:** Integrated into screamer and bloodmoon hordes after gamestage 100.
* **New Entities:** Includes various mutated forms (e.g., volcanic zombies, giant mutated animals, and more).
* **Configurable:** You can easily tweak their health, damage, and loot drops in the XML files to suit your server's difficulty.

## Installation
1. Download the latest release from the [Releases](../../releases) tab or clone the repository.
2. Extract the `MutantMonsters` folder.
3. Place the folder into your server's `Mods` directory (e.g., `SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/7 Days To Die Dedicated Server/Mods`).
4. Restart your server.

## Configuration
To adjust monster stats (health, damage, loot), edit the relevant XML files located in `MutantMonsters/Config/`.
